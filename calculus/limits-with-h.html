Evaluating Limits (as h → 0)

Master the difference quotient technique: Expand, Cancel, and Factor

Walkthrough Demonstration

Evaluate the limit using the difference quotient method:

limh→0
(x + h)3 - x3 h

Step 1: Expansion
Expand (x + h)3 using binomial expansion:

x3 + 3x2h + 3xh2 + h3 - x3 h


Step 2: Simplify and Factor
Subtract x3 - x3 and factor out h from the numerator:

h(3x2 + 3xh + h2) h


Step 3: Cancel Denominator & Substitute h = 0
Cancel the h in the denominator, leaving 3x2 + 3xh + h2.
Directly substitute h = 0 to get the final answer: 3x2.
