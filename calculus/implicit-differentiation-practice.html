Implicit Differentiation Practice

Step-by-step interactive problem sets

Level 1: Single-Term Practice

Focuses on applying the chain rule tag-along \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) to isolated \(y\) terms.

1. \(\dfrac{d}{dx}[y^5]\)

Solution Steps:

  • Apply standard power rule to the outside function: \(5y^4\)
  • Multiply immediately by the derivative of the inside function: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\)
Answer: \(5y^4 \dfrac{dy}{dx}\)
2. \(\dfrac{d}{dx}[4x^3 - 3y^4]\)

Solution Steps:

  • Differentiate the first term normally: \(\dfrac{d}{dx}[4x^3] = 12x^2\)
  • Differentiate the second term using the power rule and chain rule: \(-3\left(4y^3 \dfrac{dy}{dx}\right) = -12y^3 \dfrac{dy}{dx}\)
Answer: \(12x^2 - 12y^3 \dfrac{dy}{dx}\)
3. \(\dfrac{d}{dx}[\sin(y)]\)

Solution Steps:

  • Differentiate the outside function (sine becomes cosine): \(\cos(y)\)
  • Multiply by the derivative of the inside function: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\)
Answer: \(\cos(y) \dfrac{dy}{dx}\)

Level 2: Product Rule Practice

Focuses on handling expressions where \(x\) and \(y\) are multiplied together.

1. Find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) for: \(x^2y = 5\)

Solution Steps:

  • Apply product rule \((f \cdot g)' = f'g + fg'\) to left side: \((2x)(y) + (x^2)\left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)\)
  • Differentiate the right side constant: \(\dfrac{d}{dx}[5] = 0\)
  • Set equation: \(2xy + x^2\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 0\)
  • Isolate \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\): \(x^2\dfrac{dy}{dx} = -2xy \implies \dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{-2xy}{x^2}\)
Answer: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = -\dfrac{2y}{x}\)
2. Find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) for: \(xy^2 = 12\)

Solution Steps:

  • Apply product rule: \((1)(y^2) + (x)\left(2y \dfrac{dy}{dx}\right) = 0\)
  • Simplify: \(y^2 + 2xy\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 0\)
  • Move non-derivative term: \(2xy\dfrac{dy}{dx} = -y^2\)
  • Divide to isolate: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{-y^2}{2xy}\)
Answer: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = -\dfrac{y}{2x}\)
3. Find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) for: \(3xy - y = 4\)

Solution Steps:

  • Differentiate term by term, applying product rule to \(3xy\): \(3\left(1 \cdot y + x \cdot \dfrac{dy}{dx}\right) - \dfrac{dy}{dx} = 0\)
  • Expand: \(3y + 3x\dfrac{dy}{dx} - \dfrac{dy}{dx} = 0\)
  • Group \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) terms: \((3x - 1)\dfrac{dy}{dx} = -3y\)
  • Isolate: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{-3y}{3x - 1}\)
Answer: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = -\dfrac{3y}{3x - 1}\)

Level 3: Full Equation & Factoring

Focuses on equations where \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) appears on multiple terms and requires algebraic factoring.

1. Find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) for: \(x^2 + y^2 = 10\)

Solution Steps:

  • Differentiate all terms: \(2x + 2y\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 0\)
  • Isolate the derivative term: \(2y\dfrac{dy}{dx} = -2x\)
  • Divide both sides by \(2y\): \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{-2x}{2y}\)
Answer: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = -\dfrac{x}{y}\)
2. Find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) for: \(x^3 + y^3 = 3xy\)

Solution Steps:

  • Differentiate term by term (using product rule on right side): \(3x^2 + 3y^2\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 3\left(y + x\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right)\)
  • Expand right side: \(3x^2 + 3y^2\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 3y + 3x\dfrac{dy}{dx}\)
  • Gather all \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) terms on the left: \(3y^2\dfrac{dy}{dx} - 3x\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 3y - 3x^2\)
  • Factor out \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\): \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}(3y^2 - 3x) = 3y - 3x^2\)
  • Divide to isolate and simplify: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{3y - 3x^2}{3y^2 - 3x}\)
Answer: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{y - x^2}{y^2 - x}\)
3. Find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) for: \(y^2 - 5x = y + x^2\)

Solution Steps:

  • Differentiate every term: \(2y\dfrac{dy}{dx} - 5 = \dfrac{dy}{dx} + 2x\)
  • Collect all \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) terms on the left side: \(2y\dfrac{dy}{dx} - \dfrac{dy}{dx} = 2x + 5\)
  • Factor out \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\): \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}(2y - 1) = 2x + 5\)
  • Divide to isolate: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{2x + 5}{2y - 1}\)
Answer: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{2x + 5}{2y - 1}\)
