Logarithmic Differentiation Solution

Description / Reasons Mathematical Steps
Take the natural logarithm (\(\ln\)) of both sides to handle the variable exponent.
$$\ln(y) = \ln\left[ \left(\frac{x+1}{x-2}\right)^{x^2} \right]$$
Apply the Logarithm Power Rule: \(\ln(a^b) = b \ln(a)\).
$$\ln(y) = x^2 \ln\left(\frac{x+1}{x-2}\right)$$
Apply the Logarithm Quotient Rule: \(\ln\left(\frac{a}{b}\right) = \ln(a) - \ln(b)\).
$$\ln(y) = x^2 \left[ \ln(x+1) - \ln(x-2) \right]$$
Differentiate both sides implicitly with respect to \(x\).
$$\frac{d}{dx}[\ln(y)] = \frac{d}{dx}\left[ x^2 (\ln(x+1) - \ln(x-2)) \right]$$
Apply the Chain Rule on the left and the Product Rule on the right.
$$\frac{1}{y} \frac{dy}{dx} = x^2 \frac{d}{dx}[\ln(x+1) - \ln(x-2)] + (\ln(x+1) - \ln(x-2)) \frac{d}{dx}[x^2]$$
Evaluate individual derivatives using standard log and power rules.
$$\frac{1}{y} \frac{dy}{dx} = x^2 \left( \frac{1}{x+1} - \frac{1}{x-2} \right) + (\ln(x+1) - \ln(x-2))(2x)$$
Multiply both sides by \(y\) to isolate \(\frac{dy}{dx}\).
$$\frac{dy}{dx} = y \left[ x^2 \left(\frac{1}{x+1} - \frac{1}{x-2}\right) + 2x \ln\left(\frac{x+1}{x-2}\right) \right]$$
Substitute back \(y\) and simplify the inner fraction algebraically.
Click to view intermediate algebra steps
  • Common denominator: \((x+1)(x-2)\)
  • Numerator: \(x^2(x-2) - x^2(x+1)\)
  • Expand: \(\frac{x^3 - 2x^2 - x^3 - x^2}{(x+1)(x-2)}\)
  • Simplify: \(\frac{-3x^2}{(x+1)(x-2)}\)
$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \left(\frac{x+1}{x-2}\right)^{x^2} \left[ -\frac{3x^2}{(x+1)(x-2)} + 2x \ln\left(\frac{x+1}{x-2}\right) \right]$$
