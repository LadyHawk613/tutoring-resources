From Average Rate of Change to Instantaneous Rate

Move the second point toward the first point and watch the secant slope become the tangent slope.

Average rate of change = (f(b) − f(a)) / (b − a)
Second point: b4.00
Average rate6.00
Instantaneous rate at a4.00
Difference2.00

What do you notice?

2. Slowly decrease h toward 0.
3. Watch the second point move toward the fixed point.

Prediction

Key idea
A secant line uses two distinct points. A tangent line describes the slope at one point. We estimate the tangent slope by making the two points extremely close together.
