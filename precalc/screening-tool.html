Are You Ready for AP Precalculus?

Complete this 5-question baseline check to verify your algebraic and functional readiness.

1. Function Evaluation: If $f(x) = x^2 - 3x + 2$, what is $f(-2)$?

2. Factoring: Which expression is completely equivalent to factored form of $2x^2 - 8x - 10$?

3. Exponents & Radicals: Simplify $\sqrt{50x^4}$ assuming $x > 0$.

4. Logarithms: Evaluate $\log_3(81)$.

5. Trigonometry: In a right triangle, if the opposite side is 3 and the adjacent side is 4, what is $\tan(\theta)$?
