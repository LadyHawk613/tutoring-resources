What Is a Polynomial Function?

\[ f(x)=a_nx^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1} +\cdots+a_2x^2+a_1x+a_0 \]

A polynomial has:

  • Whole-number exponents
  • Real-number coefficients
  • No variables in denominators
  • No negative exponents
Example: \[ f(x)=3x^4-2x^3+5x-7 \]

Polynomial Vocabulary

Click each card to reveal the definition.

Degree
The highest exponent of the variable.
\[ f(x)=4x^5-2x^2+7 \] Degree = \(5\)
Leading Coefficient
The coefficient of the highest-degree term.
\[ f(x)=-3x^4+5x^2-1 \] Leading coefficient = \(-3\)
Constant Term
The term that contains no variable.
\[ f(x)=2x^3-4x+7 \] Constant = \(7\)

End Behavior

The degree and leading coefficient determine the end behavior.

Even Degree + Positive
Both ends go up.
\(x\rightarrow\pm\infty \Rightarrow f(x)\rightarrow+\infty\)
Even Degree + Negative
Both ends go down.
\(x\rightarrow\pm\infty \Rightarrow f(x)\rightarrow-\infty\)
Odd Degree + Positive
Left down, right up.
Odd Degree + Negative
Left up, right down.

Finding Zeros

\[ f(x)=x^2-9 \]

Factor the polynomial:

\[ x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3) \]

Set each factor equal to zero:

\[ x-3=0 \quad \text{and} \quad x+3=0 \]
\[ \boxed{x=3,\;-3} \]

These are the x-intercepts of the graph.

Multiplicity

Even Multiplicity
The graph touches the x-axis and turns around. \[ f(x)=(x-2)^2 \]
Odd Multiplicity
The graph crosses the x-axis. \[ f(x)=(x-2)^3 \]
