Activity 1: Function-Inverse Pair Review

Review the core parent functions and their corresponding inverse functions from your guide[cite: 1].

Inverse: Linear ($y = \frac{x - b}{m}$)

Inverse: Square Root ($y = \sqrt{x}$ for $y \ge 0$)[cite: 1]
Cubic: $y = x^3$
Inverse: Cube Root ($y = \sqrt[3]{x}$)[cite: 1]
Absolute Value: $y = |x|$
Inverse: Restricted Square Root ($y \ge 0$)[cite: 1]
Exponential: $y = b^x$
Inverse: Logarithmic ($y = \log_b(x)$)[cite: 1]
